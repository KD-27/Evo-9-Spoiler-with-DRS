This project features a custom Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX spoiler modification equipped with an active DRS (Drag Reduction System). The design aims to improve mechanical performance and test adjustable spoiler mechanics inspired by modern racing technology.

Please note: the included files do not represent exact dimensions — they are created for visualization and mechanical testing purposes only.

The Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX, known for its turbocharged power and all-wheel-drive handling, often faces challenges balancing high-speed stability and drag. This DRS-equipped spoiler concept helps tackle that by allowing dynamic adjustment of the rear wing angle, providing increased downforce during acceleration and cornering, and reduced resistance on straight lines for better speed and efficiency.
